## v1.0.0

Initial frozen public release of the cohort-aware descriptive analysis of
public human intervertebral disc degeneration transcriptomic datasets.

The release preserves the interpretation boundary of the frozen default
20-effect summary: it contains descriptive effect sizes, intervals, bootstrap
stability, and sign alignment only. It does not establish confirmatory,
mechanistic, causal, biomarker, or therapeutic conclusions.
