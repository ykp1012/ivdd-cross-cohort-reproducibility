## Novel but defensible positioning

The contribution is not a new molecular mechanism. It is a pre-specified, donor-level reproducibility audit of molecular programs across public human IVDD datasets. The project asks which reported programs are consistently associated with severity, which are cohort- or compartment-dependent, and what evidence remains after accounting for donor nesting, technical platform, and known age or grade confounding.

## Prohibited headline claims

- first discovery of NP fibrosis, AF stress cells, or AF-to-NP migration;
- a new therapeutic target or treatment recommendation;
- a causal role for a ligand, pathway, or gene;
- age-independent degeneration effect in the GSE230809 parent study;
- independent validation by the GSE229711 and GSE230808 child series, which
  belong to the same GSE230809 parent project;
- universal AF findings without an independent AF cohort.
